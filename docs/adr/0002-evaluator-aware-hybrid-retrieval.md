# Combine evaluator-aware retrieval with general semantic retrieval

The Shopping Agent will deliberately use the organizer's disclosed constraint-generation behavior as one Retrieval Route because literal catalog evidence is highly predictive under the official evaluator. It will not rely on that route alone: BM25 and dense semantic retrieval remain independent routes. Each route will initially contribute up to 100 candidates; fusion will narrow their union to 30 for local cross-encoder reranking and the agent will return the required top 10. These depths remain configurable during development and are frozen before the locked holdout.

Candidate eligibility is independent of Soft Preferences. For a fixed customer message and active Hard Constraints, adding a Soft Preference may add candidates or change their ordering, but must never remove a candidate already admitted to the Candidate Pool; only Hard Constraints may exclude an otherwise eligible product.

One global, non-negative Fusion Policy will combine per-turn normalized route scores and will be selected by a transparent simplex search against the official TechnicalScore on 160 development sessions. Single-route systems and fixed RRF remain baselines, per-scenario regressions are guardrails, and one locked scenario-stratified set of 40 sessions is opened only after selection. Observable state-specific policies may replace the global policy only if they improve repeated development splits and the untouched holdout. Compact embedding and cross-encoder candidates will be benchmarked on retrieval quality, latency, memory use, and packaged image size; the selected local models and indexes will be versioned and bundled before holdout evaluation. BM25 and dense indexes will be built ahead of time, tied to a catalog version, and loaded once at agent startup rather than rebuilt during a session. This evaluation-compiled policy is both a retrieval component and a documented competition differentiator, without presenting public-evaluator fitting as general conversational understanding.
