# Combine evaluator-aware retrieval with general semantic retrieval

The Shopping Agent will deliberately use the organizer's disclosed constraint-generation behavior as one Retrieval Route because literal catalog evidence is highly predictive under the official evaluator. It will not rely on that route alone: BM25 and dense semantic retrieval remain independent routes. Structured matching, BM25, and dense retrieval each independently admit a Route Candidate Set; the Deep Candidate Pool is their deduplicated union after Hard Constraint eligibility. No route is evidence-only, and a product need not appear in BM25 or dense results to remain eligible when structured matching admits it. Each route has a strict deterministic cap of 100 candidates, including when the cutoff splits a tied score group; tied candidates are capped by canonical parent-ASIN order because no route evidence distinguishes them. The complete three-route union is therefore bounded at 300 unique products. Fusion will rank this union for local cross-encoder reranking and the agent will return the required top 10. Slice 7 benchmarks exact independently generated pools at depths 30, 50, 100, 150, 200, 250, and 300 while reporting actual per-turn union sizes. Per-route and rerank depths are frozen before the locked holdout; an observed union size such as 100–200 is evidence, not a structural maximum.

Candidate eligibility is independent of Soft Preferences. For a fixed customer message and active Hard Constraints, adding a Soft Preference may add candidates or change their ordering, but must never remove a candidate admitted by any Retrieval Route; only Hard Constraints may exclude an otherwise eligible product.

One global, non-negative Fusion Policy will combine per-turn normalized route scores and will be selected by a transparent simplex search against the official TechnicalScore on 160 development sessions. Single-route systems and fixed RRF remain baselines, per-scenario regressions are guardrails, and one locked scenario-stratified set of 40 sessions is opened only after selection. Locked means those sessions are not executed or inspected for quality, latency, or any other configuration decision before the final human-reviewed release gate. Development reports therefore measure the complete 160-session development run and label any 200-session runtime figure as a projection; they never obtain that figure by running the holdout early. A reranker configuration is admissible only when its projected complete 200-session wall time is at most 900 seconds and its measured p95 added rerank latency is at most 1.5 seconds per turn. Both limits are mandatory gates applied before quality comparison, so acceptable aggregate runtime cannot hide tail-latency failures and acceptable tail latency cannot hide an impractical complete run. Among admissible configurations, Slice 7 rejects any configuration that reduces HitRate@10 or fails to improve replay TechnicalScore over fused-30, then freezes the greatest remaining rerank depth. At that depth it selects the model with the highest replay TechnicalScore, breaking ties by MRR, lower p95 latency, and smaller packaged size. Depth takes priority after the runtime and quality floors because later Fusion Policy training cannot recover candidates excluded by a prematurely shallow boundary. If the declared candidates produce no admissible configuration, one predeclared contingency round may benchmark smaller cross-encoders through the identical gate. The limits are never relaxed after results are visible; if the contingency also fails, Slice 7 freezes fused-30 with no local cross-encoder and records that outcome rather than manufacturing a winner. Observable state-specific policies may replace the global policy only if they improve repeated development splits and the untouched holdout. Compact embedding and cross-encoder candidates will be benchmarked on retrieval quality, latency, memory use, and packaged image size; the selected local models and indexes will be versioned and bundled before holdout evaluation. BM25 and dense indexes will be built ahead of time, tied to a catalog version, and loaded once at agent startup rather than rebuilt during a session. This evaluation-compiled policy is both a retrieval component and a documented competition differentiator, without presenting public-evaluator fitting as general conversational understanding.
